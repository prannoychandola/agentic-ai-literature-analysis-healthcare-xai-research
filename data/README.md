# Data

`corpus.csv` is the retrieved and relevance-screened healthcare-XAI literature corpus used by the experiments. It contains paper metadata from Semantic Scholar and arXiv, including source identifiers, titles, abstracts where available, authors, dates, DOI values, URLs, and citation counts where provided.

The corpus contains 187 unique papers. Papers without usable abstracts remain in the corpus for accounting and metadata reporting, but they are excluded from embedding-based semantic analysis.

Before publishing or redistributing `corpus.csv`, inspect the licensing, terms of use, and redistribution requirements of the original scholarly sources. This repository does not assert a license for the retrieved paper metadata or abstracts.
