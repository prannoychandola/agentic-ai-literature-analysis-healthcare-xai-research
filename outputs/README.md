# Outputs

This directory contains experimental artifacts generated by the pipeline. Files should be regenerated only with the documented configuration and should not be manually edited as research results.

## Major Files

- `corpus_stats.json`: retrieval, screening, deduplication, metadata, and source statistics.
- `rejected_papers.csv`: papers rejected by the deterministic relevance screen and their reasons.
- `corpus_manifest.csv`: final corpus index and abstract availability.
- `retrieval_progress.json`: progressive retrieval checkpoint.
- `embeddings.npy`: normalized MiniLM embedding matrix for papers with usable abstracts.
- `embedding_index.csv`: mapping from embedding row to paper ID and metadata.
- `clustering_results.json`: K-Means metrics for K=2..8 and the selected K.
- `cluster_assignments.csv`: selected semantic cluster assignment for each embedded paper.
- `baseline_results.json` and `baseline_summary.csv`: MiniLM, TF-IDF, and TF-IDF plus SVD comparison metrics.
- `ablation_results.json` and `ablation_summary.csv`: title-only, abstract-only, and title-plus-abstract ablation metrics.
- `stability_results.json`, `stability_summary.csv`, and stability CSV files: multi-seed clustering robustness and ARI/NMI comparisons.
- `gap_analysis.json`: evidence-traceable candidate research-gap hypotheses and limitations.
- `gap_candidates.csv`: tabular candidate-gap summaries.
- `gap_evidence.csv`: exact abstract evidence passages associated with candidates.

## Figures

The `figures/` directory contains descriptive plots for clustering metrics, baselines, ablations, stability, and evidence support. Clustering scores are descriptive evaluation results; they do not establish semantic validity or prove research gaps.

Candidate research gaps are hypotheses supported by extracted corpus evidence, not proven or confirmed gaps. They require further investigation and domain review.
